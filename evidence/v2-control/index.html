When will Auralis ANC Pro ship?

Pre-orders are planned to begin shipping in October 2026. As soon as your unit leaves the warehouse, you will receive a tracking link by email. Shipping times vary by region, but most orders arrive within 3–10 business days after dispatch.

Can I use Auralis ANC Pro wired without battery?

Yes. You can connect the included 3.5 mm audio cable to listen in passive wired mode when the battery is low or when wireless connections are not allowed. Active features like ANC and transparency require power, but audio will still pass through over the cable.

Does it work well for calls and meetings?

Auralis ANC Pro uses a dedicated voice microphone array and noise suppression to keep your speech clear on calls. It is designed to work smoothly with popular meeting tools on laptops and phones, so you can move between music and meetings without switching devices.

How does the 30-day listening guarantee work?

From the day your headphones are delivered, you have 30 days to listen in your own spaces. If you decide they are not the right match, you can request a return, send them back in original condition, and receive a refund according to the return policy for your region.

Is there an app for customization?

A companion app is planned to offer EQ presets, ANC mode control, and firmware updates. At launch, you will be able to use the on-device controls for core features, with additional customization rolling out over time.

What about comfort for glasses wearers?

The ear cushions use soft memory foam and a flexible seal designed to accommodate most glasses frames. Clamp force has been tuned to stay secure while reducing pressure points, so you can listen comfortably through long sessions.
